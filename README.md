## 🌟 About Jami at-Tirmidhi

**Jami' at-Tirmidhi (جامع الترمذي)**, compiled by **Imam Abu Isa Muhammad at-Tirmidhi (rahimahullah)**, is famous for its unique legal commentary (Fiqh), ethical guidance, and precise grading of authentic Hadiths.

This Flutter app provides the complete collection of **Jami at-Tirmidhi Hadiths** with side-by-side Urdu & English translations, fast offline search, and dark theme support.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📜 **Complete Collection**: All books and chapters of Jami' at-Tirmidhi.
- 🌐 **Dual Translations**: Accurate **Urdu** (جامع الترمذي اردو) and **English** translations.
- ⚡ **Fast Search**: Sub-50ms offline search by Hadith number or search terms.
- 📡 **100% Offline**: Embedded database with zero network overhead.
- 📌 **Bookmarks**: Save important Hadiths and study notes.
